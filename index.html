The space

Your own little house, not a spare room.

Completed in 2026, this detached guesthouse sits privately behind its own entrance on a quiet, safe street. Inside you'll find a bright open living area with high ceilings, a fully stocked kitchen, a dining nook and a coffee station ready for slow mornings.

Two bedrooms (a king and a queen) plus a sofa bed in the living room make it easy for families or a group of friends. Two full bathrooms mean no morning line-up, and the in-unit washer and dryer means you can pack light.
